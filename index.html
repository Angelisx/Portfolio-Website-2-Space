About Me

I'm Angel Smythe, a web developer who likes building things that feel a little more alive than the average page — interactive 3D scenes, small game-like touches, and interfaces that reward a bit of curiosity.

My day-to-day toolkit spans JavaScript, Three.js, and modern frameworks like Next.js, and I deploy most of my projects on Netlify so I can iterate quickly and ship often. This portfolio is itself one of those projects — equal parts resume and playground.

When I'm not shipping side projects, I'm usually chasing down a new idea worth prototyping. Take a look around the Projects section to see a few of them.
